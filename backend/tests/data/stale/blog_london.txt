Kestrel Labs opens its first London office

Posted 22 September 2026 by the Kestrel Labs team

Kestrel Labs has opened its first office outside Dublin, in London's Shoreditch. The office will be home to our new sales team and to customer success for UK customers.

"Half of our pipeline already comes from the UK," said chief executive Aoife Byrne. "Being in London lets us meet those customers in person."
