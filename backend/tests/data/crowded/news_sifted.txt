Stockholm's Lumen Analytics lands $40M Series B led by Northzone

10 October 2026

Product analytics startup Lumen Analytics has closed a $40M Series B round led by Northzone. Point Nine and Seedcamp also took part.

Lumen, founded in 2021, helps mid-market SaaS companies see which product features drive upgrades. The company plans to use the funding to expand its sales and marketing teams in North America, according to co-founder Maya Lindqvist.

The raise is one of the larger Nordic B2B software rounds this quarter.
