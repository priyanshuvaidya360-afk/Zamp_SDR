Welcome, Daniel Reyes: our first Chief Revenue Officer

Posted by the Lumen Analytics team

We're excited to welcome Daniel Reyes as Lumen Analytics' first Chief Revenue Officer. Daniel joins from a decade of building sales teams at B2B software companies, most recently as VP Sales at a Series C analytics company.

Daniel will lead sales, customer success and revenue operations, and will be based in our Austin office. "Lumen's customers love the product. My job is to make sure the next thousand companies hear about it," Daniel said.

Daniel started on 18 August 2026.
