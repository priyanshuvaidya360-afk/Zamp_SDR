Lumen Analytics raises $40M Series B to bring product analytics to mid-market SaaS

October 10, 2026

Lumen Analytics, the product analytics company for mid-market SaaS teams, has raised a $40 million Series B led by Northzone, with participation from existing investors Point Nine and Seedcamp. The round brings Lumen's total funding to $58 million.

Chief executive Maya Lindqvist said the money will go into go-to-market: "We built the product with 300 customers who found us on their own. Now we need to go and find the next 3,000."

Lumen sells to product and growth teams at software companies with 50 to 500 employees. The company says revenue tripled over the past year. It has 120 employees across Stockholm and Austin.
