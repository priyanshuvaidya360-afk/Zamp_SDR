Acme Fintech raises $40M Series C to automate finance operations for Indian SMBs
October 2, 2026

Bengaluru-based Acme Fintech has raised $40 million in a Series C round led by Lightspeed, with participation from existing investors Sequoia and Accel. The company said it will use the funds to double its engineering team and expand into Southeast Asia over the next 12 months.

"Finance teams at mid-sized companies are still running on spreadsheets," said Arjun Mehta, co-founder and CEO. Acme Fintech now serves more than 3,000 businesses.
