The Finance Leaders Podcast, Episode 42: Scaling AP without scaling headcount
Released October 8, 2026

This week we talk to Rohan Gupta, CFO of Acme Fintech. Rohan says: "We are drowning in invoices. Our accounts payable volume tripled this year and we had to double the AP team just to keep up."

Rohan also mentions that VP Finance Priya Raman is leading a project to rebuild their approval workflows before the next audit.
